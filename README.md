# Space Rescue

A web-based space-rescue game. Pilot a servicing vehicle through a debris field, reach a damaged satellite, and repair it before your propellant runs out.

**Play online:** https://archit-ravikumar.github.io/space-rescue-game/

## About

Debris from a retired rocket stage has struck a chain of five communications satellites. You fly to each one, hold position inside its docking range until the repair completes, and move on to the next mission.

- Reach the satellite and finish docking: **SATELLITE SUCCESSFULLY RESCUED!**
- Crash on your last life or run out of propellant: **MISSION FAILED!**

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Thrust | Arrow keys or WASD |
| Boost (uses more propellant) | Shift or Space |
| Debris pulse | E |
| Pause | P |
| Mute | M |
| Touch devices | Drag to fly |

## Features

- Player spacecraft, damaged satellite, moving asteroids and a space background with an Earth horizon
- Rescue zone around the satellite with a docking progress ring
- Propellant meter with grey canisters and a full-refill tank
- 3 lives, mission timer, score and saved best score
- Deflector shields, debris pulses, fast debris with warnings, and gravity anomalies
- 5 missions, each with denser and faster debris
- Sound effects, mission briefing, and live velocity and range telemetry

## Space exploration link

Real missions must repair or service failing satellites while managing limited fuel and avoiding orbital debris. Orbit Rescue turns those same challenges into a short game.

## What makes it unique

Momentum-based flight, propellant management, a docking mechanic you must hold, debris pulses, and five missions of rising difficulty.

## Built with

HTML, CSS and JavaScript (HTML5 canvas and Web Audio). It is a single file with no external game engine.
